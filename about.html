ABOUT SATGOLD CLOUD

Helping school teams work in sync.

Connected tools for school administration, teaching and family communication.

A PRODUCT BY SATGOLD TECHNOL

One clearer place for school work

SatGold Cloud is being developed as a multi-school management platform for school leaders, teachers, students and parents. It brings school records, academic workflows and family updates together while giving each school a separate, role-based workspace.

Built around useful routines

School software should support everyday work that keeps learning moving: knowing who is in class, sharing lesson resources, reviewing student progress and keeping families informed. SatGold Cloud is designed around those routines.

Feature availability depends on platform configuration and connected services. Contact hello@satgoldcloud.name.ng with questions.
