Privacy policy

Draft privacy notice · Updated 12 October 2026

A TRANSPARENT STARTING POINT

Controller and service details

SatGold Cloud is a school management service by SatGold Technol. This draft must be completed and reviewed by the service owner before production use. The owner must identify the legal entity, contact details, privacy contact and applicable processing roles.

Information in the service

The service may process user account details, school membership information, student and guardian records, academic records, attendance, communications, documents and subscription records. Schools determine which records they enter and are responsible for their lawful collection and accuracy.

Use and service providers

Information supports school operations and authorized communications. Planned providers include Supabase for authentication, database and storage; Vercel for hosting; and optional payment, email and AI providers. Before enabling any provider, the operator must document data shared, configure controls and review provider terms. Student information should not be sent to AI unless the school authorizes the use and the data is minimized.

Retention, security and rights

Retention periods, lawful bases, international transfers, incident handling, and processes for access, correction, deletion and complaints must be defined by the service owner and participating schools. Contact the school that manages your account for requests about its records.

Contact and publication note

Contact hello@satgoldcloud.name.ng with questions. Complete organization details and review this draft against applicable Nigerian data protection requirements before launch.
