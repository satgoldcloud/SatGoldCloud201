A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Still have a question about getting your school set up? Talk with our team ↗

What is SatGold Cloud?+

SatGold Cloud is a school management platform designed to bring administration, academic records, assessments and family communication into one connected workspace.

Can different schools use the platform separately?+

Each school has its own workspace and school ID. Database access policies are designed to scope private records to authorized members of that school.

Can parents and students see reports?+

Families can see relevant results after the school has reviewed and published them. Unapproved report drafts remain private to authorized school staff.

Is there a public page for my school?+

Each registered school can have a public profile page with approved school information. Sensitive student and staff records remain private.

How do I choose a plan?+

Starter and Premium provide monthly packages, and eligible schools can access a one-month trial. Contact us to discuss a tailored Custom plan.
